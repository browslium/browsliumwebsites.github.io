The platform

One control plane for managed-device security.

Browslium brings enrollment, policy, application distribution, protected connectivity, compliance, and support into one managed service.

01

Device enrollment

Enroll supported devices through Android Enterprise, Apple automated management, Windows Autopilot or MDM, and other platform-authorized enrollment paths.

02

Always-on protection

Use the platform-appropriate protected path: managed VPN, global proxy, or signed Windows network enforcement. Unsupported direct routes can be blocked when policy requires fail-closed protection.

03

Apps and policies

Distribute the disclosed Browslium components, certificates, and approved applications, then apply the authorized customer’s device and security policies.

04

Content security

Evaluate supported URLs, webpage text, images, video, audio, downloads, and documents and return an allow, block, replace, or review decision before supported content is delivered.

05

Compliance visibility

Give authorized administrators the device, application, policy, security, and compliance status needed to operate their managed fleet.

06

Direct customer support

Browslium provides every customer with direct email support for onboarding, deployment, troubleshooting, privacy, and offboarding.

Windows protection

Network-wide enforcement without browser extensions.

The planned Windows production architecture uses Windows Filtering Platform enforcement, a transparent local HTTPS proxy, and a managed certificate authority. Core filtering does not require a Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or other browser extension.

Global Windows layer

Browsers and compatible applications.

  • A production-signed Windows Filtering Platform component will enforce the approved network path.
  • A transparent local HTTPS proxy and trusted Browslium certificate will process supported encrypted traffic.
  • Standard traffic from browsers, WebView2, Electron, and compatible native applications can use the same filtering pipeline.
  • Unauthorized direct connections, tunneling, or unsupported bypass paths can be blocked instead of silently allowed.

Pre-delivery content layer

One protected path for compatible browsers and applications.

  • Supported HTTPS responses can be inspected and rewritten before content reaches the browser or application.
  • Security headers and content rules can prevent disallowed media from loading while a decision is pending.
  • Standard compatible browsers use the same protected network path, regardless of browser brand.
  • The same protected network architecture serves every compatible browser brand.

Some applications use certificate pinning, digital-rights management, or end-to-end encryption that prevents inspection. Approved critical services may receive a narrowly scoped bypass; unsupported content applications may remain blocked rather than operate without filtering. See the Privacy Policy.

Deployment lifecycle

Authority and transparency come first.

Every deployment begins with verified ownership or management authority and clear notice to the people who use the managed devices.

01 / Agreement

Confirm authority

The customer confirms that the customer owns or is legally authorized to manage each device and accepts the applicable service and data terms.

02 / Notice

Inform users

Users receive clear disclosure about device management, required applications, network processing, policy enforcement, and available support.

03 / Enrollment

Apply protection

Supported devices enroll, receive management policies, install required applications, and activate the customer’s protected connection.

04 / Offboarding

Remove responsibly

An adult account holder, device owner, or authorized administrator deprovisions, wipes, transfers, or retires the device according to its ownership model and the customer’s instructions.

What Browslium is

Managed security with disclosed controls.

  • A commercial managed-device and content-security service for individuals, families, organizations, and institutions.
  • A management system for devices owned by or expressly authorized to the customer under the supported platform plan.
  • A platform for application distribution, policy enforcement, compliance, and direct support.
  • A service governed by customer agreements, appropriate user notice, and privacy disclosures.

What Browslium is not

No hidden or payment-based control.

  • Not a device-financing, leasing, debt-collection, or payment-enforcement product.
  • Not used to lock, wipe, disable, or restrict a device because a payment is late or missing.
  • Not a covert monitoring, fingerprinting, advertising, or eavesdropping system.
  • Not a method for installing undisclosed applications or altering devices for resale.
