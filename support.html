Before you contact us

Include enough detail to reproduce the issue.

  • Customer, family, or organization name and an authorized contact.
  • Device manufacturer, model, ownership mode, and operating-system version.
  • Browslium application version and enrollment method.
  • On Windows: edition and build, affected browser or application and version, and connection type.
  • Time the issue occurred, including time zone.
  • Exact error, affected app or site, and steps already attempted.

Never send by email

Keep credentials and sensitive data out of the ticket.

  • Passwords, one-time codes, recovery codes, or authentication secrets.
  • Windows product keys, complete Autopilot hardware hashes, or device recovery keys.
  • Private keys, API keys, service-account keys, signing certificates, or certificate-authority secrets.
  • Full credit-card, bank-account, medical, or government-identity details.
  • Unredacted content unrelated to the problem.
