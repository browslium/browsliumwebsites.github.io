3. Device enrollment and management

3.1 Supported ownership model

Browslium’s initial Android enterprise management offering is intended for organization-owned devices configured for work use in Fully Managed mode. Enrollment may require a factory reset. Personally owned or consumer devices may not be enrolled in this enterprise-only mode unless Browslium expressly supports an appropriate ownership model and the deployment is permitted by platform rules, contract, and law.

A supported Windows deployment may include a customer-owned computer only when its owner voluntarily grants documented device-management and content-filtering authority and the enrollment method, license, agreement, and applicable law permit that model. Windows protection requires a compatible, genuine, activated, and properly licensed edition identified in the applicable service description; the initial Windows plan is Windows 11 Pro. Browslium does not provide, share, reuse, bypass, or circumvent a Windows product activation license.

3.2 Enrollment methods

Supported Android devices may be provisioned through a QR code, enrollment token, zero-touch enrollment, Samsung Knox Mobile Enrollment, or another platform-supported method. Apple devices may use Apple’s supported automated or manual management paths. Eligible Windows devices may use Windows Autopilot, Microsoft Entra, MDM, a documented provisioning package, or another Microsoft-supported enrollment method. The applicable platform management component may be installed and bound to the authorized Customer record.

3.3 Required applications and policies

The Customer authorizes Browslium to distribute the disclosed Browslium applications and approved managed applications, configure certificates and network settings, enforce the protected connection and fail-closed rules where selected, apply device restrictions, obtain compliance information, and issue authorized management commands according to the Customer’s policy.

On Windows, required components may include the Browslium local agent and service, a production driver approved and signed through Microsoft’s Windows driver process, a Windows Filtering Platform enforcement component, a transparent local HTTPS proxy, and a managed certificate authority. Core filtering does not require a browser extension. Removing, disabling, downgrading, or bypassing a required component may cause the affected connection, browser, or application to remain blocked until compliant operation is restored; Browslium does not impose an unrelated whole-computer payment lock. Some Windows production capabilities remain under development until required technical, signing, licensing, and platform approvals are complete.

A compatible device that remains assigned through Android zero-touch or manufacturer enrollment, Apple automated device enrollment, or Windows Autopilot may automatically return to management after a reset. A manually enrolled device or a materially changed device may require an administrator to repeat enrollment.

The Customer is responsible for maintaining and eventually removing applicable zero-touch, Knox, Apple automated enrollment, reseller, manufacturer, Windows Autopilot, Entra, and MDM assignments when a device is lawfully transferred, replaced, released, or retired.
