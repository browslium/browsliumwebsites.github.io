Product Privacy Notice

This notice describes the Browslium product account, portal and managed-device features when they are enabled. It is separate from the privacy policy for this informational website. Features and data flows may differ by device and development stage.

Published October 9, 2026

Privacy policy for this website ↗

Who is responsible

Browslium LLC, 30 N Gould St, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA, is the contact for this product notice. Send privacy questions or requests to admin@browslium.com.

Who can hold an account

Product accounts are for adults only. Registration asks the person creating an account to declare that they are an adult; it does not currently use an age-verification service. Acknowledging receipt of this notice is distinct from any optional consent, which must be requested separately when needed. An adult may manage a device used by another person only with the required ownership, authority and permissions. Browslium does not offer accounts to minors. If you believe a minor created an account, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

Account and identity data

For account creation and sign-in, we may handle your name, email address, account identifiers, organization or role, authentication events and session data. If you choose Google or Microsoft sign-in, the identity provider sends the information needed to identify your account through OpenID Connect. Its own privacy terms also apply to its service. Do not send us your Google or Microsoft password.

Devices, settings and requests

When an adult enrolls or manages an authorized device, the product may handle device identifiers and technical characteristics, enrollment and connection status, software version, assigned policies, per-device settings, administrative actions, exception requests and their outcomes. Device management may involve information about a device user other than the account holder. The account holder must have the appropriate authority to configure that device and inform affected people as required.

Filtering and technical data

Filtering supported content requires analysis to decide which selected policy applies. Depending on the device, feature and deployment, this may involve content or derived signals, URLs or categories, decision results, error reports and security logs. Do not assume that content is never transmitted or retained. We will update this notice with verified feature-specific processing and retention details before general service access.

Payments and messages

If payment setup is offered, Stripe handles payment-card entry and storage through its own interface. Browslium may receive customer, payment-method reference, setup or billing status and transaction records needed to administer the account. We do not ask you to enter full card numbers in the Browslium portal. Service messages may be sent through Resend; we process the recipient address, message and delivery information needed for those communications. If you contact support, we receive what you choose to send.

Why we use information

We use the information described above to provide sign-in and account access, enroll and administer authorized devices, apply selected filtering policies, respond to exceptions and support requests, communicate about the service, administer payment setup and billing where available, protect the service, investigate errors and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. We do not describe the product as having no data processing.

Service providers and locations

Google and Microsoft may provide sign-in, Resend may deliver email, Stripe may process payment setup or payments, and DigitalOcean may host service infrastructure. Each receives information relevant to its role. Processing may occur outside your country, including in the United States. Additional integrations or locations must be disclosed when actually enabled; this notice does not claim a verified international transfer mechanism for every jurisdiction.

Retention and security

The data needed to operate an account or managed device, respond to requests, secure the service and satisfy applicable records duties may be retained for different periods. Exact schedules, backup behavior and feature-specific retention remain to be verified against the deployed system and will be published before general service access. We use access controls and other safeguards appropriate to the implemented service, but no system can promise absolute security.

Your choices and requests

Account holders can choose supported policies and manage authorized devices and settings where the feature is available. For access, correction, deletion or another privacy request, email admin@browslium.com. We may need to verify identity and authority, and some information may need to be retained where required or permitted by applicable law. Available rights depend on your location and the circumstances.

Changes and scope

We will revise the publication date and this notice when material product data practices change. This page describes the product; the separate website policy covers visits to browslium.com and voluntary contributions made through the informational site. Publication of this notice does not mean every product feature is generally available.
