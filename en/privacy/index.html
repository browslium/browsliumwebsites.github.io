Privacy Policy

This policy covers the public Browslium website. Browslium’s filtering service is in development and testing; this site does not itself inspect your browsing content.

Published October 6, 2026

01Who we are02Browsing this site03Language preference04Contact by email05Voluntary contributions06Future filtering service07Requests, security and changes

Who we are

Browslium LLC operates this website. For a privacy question or request, write to admin@browslium.com.

Browsing this site

The site is a static website. We have not installed advertising trackers or a third-party analytics script. The hosting provider may process technical data, such as your IP address, browser, requested page and time, to deliver and protect the site. We do not use this website to inspect the content of other sites you visit.

Language preference

The site reads your browser language settings to offer a one-time language suggestion. If you select a language, your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage so it takes priority on later visits. Dismissing the suggestion is also saved locally. These settings are not sent to Browslium by the language feature, and you can remove them using your browser’s site-data controls.

Contact by email

A contact link opens your own email application. If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to send. Avoid passwords, payment-card numbers and other sensitive material. We use your message to answer or investigate your request and may keep correspondence while needed for that purpose or legal obligations. Ask us about access or deletion at the same address.

Voluntary contributions

The donation button opens a payment page hosted by Stripe. Stripe handles payment-card information under its own privacy terms. This website does not collect or store your card details. Browslium may receive contribution records and contact details that Stripe makes available to the account holder to administer contributions, accounting or required records. A contribution does not buy product access.

Future filtering service

The planned product needs to analyze supported content to make filtering decisions. This website does not activate that processing. Before general access to the service, we will publish separate, accurate information about product data flows, retention, security and user choices based on the production system. We do not claim that the future service processes nothing or stores nothing.

Requests, security and changes

You may contact us about information you provided by email or through a contribution. We may need to verify a request and keep records required by law. Data may be processed by our hosting, email or payment providers in other countries. No website can promise absolute security. If this policy changes materially, we will update its date and content.
