Browslium / Video content filtering

Video filtering follows what changes over time

A video is more than its page address or thumbnail. Browslium is being designed to evaluate supported moving content over time using a separate video policy.

Explore interactive levels ↓Platforms

Why video needs its own policy

Scenes and subjects change from frame to frame. A decision based only on a URL or a single still image can miss what appears later. Browslium therefore defines six video policies separately from its seven image policies.

How the design handles video

The intended filtering path analyzes supported video content across time, compares detections with the selected level, and applies the corresponding treatment. Actual coverage, timing and behavior depend on the platform and implementation stage.

Video content filtering

The six video policy levels

The descriptions below come from the project’s current policy document. They describe intended behavior, not general production availability.

  1. 01

    Mask women completely in the video.

  2. 02

    Mask women and women’s clothing completely in the video.

  3. 03

    Mask women and alter the remaining shape in the video.

  4. 04

    Mask women and women’s clothing, with shape alteration in the video.

  5. 05

    Block the entire video when it contains women.

  6. 06

    Block the entire video when it contains women or women’s clothing.
