Browslium / Image content filtering

Image filtering that looks at the image

A permitted page can still contain an image that does not match someone’s chosen standard. Browslium is being built to evaluate supported images individually and apply the selected image policy.

Explore interactive levels ↗Platform status
01

Why a website address is not enough

A domain or URL says where content comes from, but it does not describe every image on a page. Where a platform supports it, an image-level decision can affect one item without automatically making the same decision for the whole page.

02

From image to policy decision

A supported image enters the filtering path, is analyzed, and is compared with the chosen policy. The policy determines whether the item is shown, masked, altered or blocked. Exact handling and coverage depend on the platform and current implementation.

Image content filtering

The seven image policy levels

These are the project’s current policy definitions. A listed policy is not a claim that it is available in a released product.

  1. 01

    Mask visible features of women while leaving clothing visible.

  2. 02

    Mask women completely.

  3. 03

    Mask women and women’s clothing completely.

  4. 04

    Mask women completely and alter the remaining shape.

  5. 05

    Mask women and women’s clothing, with shape alteration.

  6. 06

    Block an entire image when it contains women.

  7. 07

    Block an entire image when it contains women or women’s clothing.

Browslium

Current availability

Browslium remains in development and testing. The Android pilot does not yet run production AI filtering; Apple and Windows builds also require release work. Check the platform status before assuming an image policy is active on a device.
