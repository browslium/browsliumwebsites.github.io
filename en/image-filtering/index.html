Why a website address is not enough

A domain or URL says where content comes from, but it does not describe every image on a page. Where a platform supports it, an image-level decision can affect one item without automatically making the same decision for the whole page.

From image to policy decision

A supported image enters the filtering path, is analyzed, and is compared with the chosen policy. The policy determines whether the item is shown, masked, altered or blocked. Exact handling and coverage depend on the platform and current implementation.

Current availability

Browslium remains in development and testing. The Android pilot does not yet run production AI filtering; Apple and Windows builds also require release work. Check the platform status before assuming an image policy is active on a device.

Audio requires its own filtering path and remains in development. Visible text passes through unchanged, without semantic analysis, even when a GPU is available.
